🧮Worked example

Say you generate roughly 100,000 characters (≈ a short book) of speech in a month.

Fly.io (this app): cost is driven by machine-seconds, not characters. Kokoro-82M generates audio in a few seconds per request, so 100k characters spread across normal-sized requests is typically a few minutes of total compute — often just cents, thanks to scale-to-zero. If you instead pin min_machines_running = 1 to avoid cold starts, you pay the flat ≈ $12–$14/mo regardless of how much (or little) you generate.
Google AI Studio / Gemini API: ≈100,000 characters is roughly 25,000 input tokens → ≈ $0.0125 in text-input cost on Flash TTS. The dominant cost is the audio output tokens ($10.00 / 1M), which scale with spoken duration rather than character count — so the total depends on pacing and voice. Use the official pricing page to model your exact duration, or just generate for free inside the AI Studio playground ✨🔊 first.

Rule of thumb: for occasional/low-volume speech generation, Google AI Studio's free tier and pay-per-character API are cheaper and require zero ops. For steady, high-volume, or privacy-sensitive workloads, a scale-to-zero Fly.io deployment like this one flattens cost and keeps data in your own infra.
